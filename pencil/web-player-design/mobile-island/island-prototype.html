Not shelves. A signal.

A mobile player concept where the navigation is an island that carries playback.

  1. The island is the player. Tabs, artwork with a progress ring and play live in one floating shape. There is no strip stacked on a tab bar.
  2. Home resumes, then tunes. It opens on where you stopped and on which device. A dial by feeling replaces "Good evening" and the tile grid.
  3. Bento, not shelves. The tiles have different sizes and jobs: friends listening now, an artist's own note, offline space, your week in sound.
  4. Jam lives in Library. Listening together sits next to what you saved, as a filter with live Jams on top — no extra tab. Moving playback between devices is Bitrate Connect, one tap from the player.
  5. Moments on the waveform. Friends' reactions sit on the track timeline.
  6. The name is visible. A live quality badge (format, bit depth, kbps) and the output device sit next to the controls.
