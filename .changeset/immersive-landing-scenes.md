The landing page is a full-screen sequence of eleven scenes with keyboard, wheel, dot and swipe navigation, a WebGL backdrop with 2D fallbacks and a static reduced-motion version. Guests are offered "Create account" and "Log in" and are never sent into the player; signed-in visitors get "Open web player". The landing styles moved to Tailwind and design tokens.
