Redesigned Catalog, Albums, Playlists, Podcasts and Genres on the glass building blocks, in English and Ukrainian. Each list gained KPI tiles read from the list endpoints (pipeline states for tracks; live and taken-down counts and, for operators who can read the moderation queue, open reports for albums, playlists and podcast episodes), filter menus, a search box, phone cards and distinct empty, no-results and failed states. Track, album, playlist and podcast pages became bentos of tiles; the genre editor shows a live preview and its usage, and can delete an unused genre. Write failures, processing history, audio errors and form validation are translated too.
