Error, empty and not-found screens use the design system's State/Message: a 72px icon circle (red for errors), an Unbounded title and primary/outline actions — including the app error boundaries, search and podcasts. Playlists explain an expired session (with Log in), a private playlist, a missing one and rate limits; playlists, albums and Liked Songs load with a cover and track-row skeleton. Albums get a Play button and a heart in their action bar, show the release year in the header and leave out Date added.
