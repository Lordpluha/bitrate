The desktop app follows the design system's shell: a 40px top bar with the Bitrate wordmark and a 420px search, one rounded workspace card holding the library, the page and Now Playing, and a floating 88px player card. The library panel gains Home, Search, Liked Songs and Recents navigation; Now Playing shows the artwork, title and the next three tracks; the player bar uses the primary 46px play button, a 3px seek rail and moves Bitrate Connect and the floating player beside the volume.
