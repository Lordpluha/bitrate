The player now shows the playing track on the lock screen and in the OS media controls and answers the media keys (`media-session`), starts the next track just before the current one ends instead of after a gap, and lets listeners pick the stream quality or leave it on Auto, a pick that holds for the following tracks. In web-player the quality picker sits in the now-playing view, Shift+←/→ skips 10 seconds, L likes the track, Q opens the queue, + and − change the volume, and screen readers hear which track is now playing.
