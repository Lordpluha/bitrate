The landing works on phones and short laptops and leads more visitors into the player. It is nine scenes instead of eleven. On a phone each scene fits one screen: the copy sits on top and the main block fills the rest, scrolling inside it, and a swipe past the block's end moves on. A bottom bar keeps "Start listening — it's free" and a next-scene button within thumb reach. On short laptops (1366×768, 1280×720) the scenes are fitted to the width and the page scrolls, instead of everything being drawn small. Each demo scene links to the player. Every button and link in the web player now shows the pointer cursor.
