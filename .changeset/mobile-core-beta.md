The mobile app became a player: Home with resume, a quick grid, recommendation rows and recently
played; search with type filters; Library and Liked Songs; Profile with theme (System, Dark,
Light, Neutral) and language (English, Українська, Polski) choices. Navigation and playback live
in the floating Mobile Island, and a full-screen Now Playing view seeks and skips. Tracks play
through react-native-track-player (an `<audio>` element on web) from the API's play sessions;
tracks with only encrypted renditions say they cannot play on mobile yet.
