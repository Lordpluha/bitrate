Added the Promotion stage to the release workspace for artists with AI promotion enabled. It covers
every state from "set up your profile" and "pick a release date" to generating, failed and the
plan itself: dated steps from four weeks before to four weeks after the release, grouped by phase,
each approved, edited, rejected, regenerated with a hint or marked done by the artist, with the
"Created with AI" marker on AI text. POST steps open their drafts: one English master per channel
to edit, copy or download, and Ukrainian and Polish machine translations to review, edit and rate.
The plan summary, promotion profile and monthly AI allowance sit beside the plan, and the Launch
stage's promotion row now opens the stage.
