Artists can now publish an approved release on Bitrate from the Launch stage, right away or as a premiere at the planned time, take it offline and publish it again. A release goes live all at once: its tracks stay hidden from listeners until every recording has been converted, then the album and tracks appear together and followers get one notification. The workspace links to the album in the player once the release is live. Admins can retry a failed publication and take a live release down; a release taken down by Bitrate cannot be republished by the artist. Artists can also delete a release draft. Track and album responses now include `publishedAt`.
