Profiles follow the design: a 180px round avatar beside a 72px name and the bio, with Edit profile and Settings on your own profile and an outline Follow button on someone else's. Your profile is a bento of tiles — top artists, top tracks (with the right columns), your playlists, following artists and the people you follow — and public profiles show their playlists as design cards with followers and following as avatars. Loading, signed-out and not-found use the design system's states.
