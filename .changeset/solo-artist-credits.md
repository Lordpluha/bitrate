Accuracy is now confirmed once, by submitting the release for review, instead of in the rights form. A new release draft now credits its artist as performer, composer and lyricist with 100% of the master and publishing splits, so a solo release needs no credit or split work before review. A draft without credits, such as one created earlier, offers "I made it myself" to add the same credit in one step. Contributors can now be removed from a draft; their shares go with them. The release timing is now chosen in the draft — right away, or a premiere on a date and time in UTC — and can still be changed after Bitrate approves the release; Deliver is marked optional once approved. Select menus in the workspace keep readable options in every theme and their arrow clear of the edge.
