The sign-in, 2FA, sign-up, password recovery and email verification screens follow the new Pencil design: a full-screen brand panel beside the form (sign-in, 2FA, sign-up) or one centred card (recovery, verification), the Unbounded/Inter brand type, labelled fields with inline validation, a six-cell 2FA code field, and inline alerts for a failed sign-in, a sent reset link and a broken reset link instead of toasts. Social sign-in shows Google, Facebook and Apple up front and folds the other providers behind "More options". The auth screens no longer render the public header and footer.
