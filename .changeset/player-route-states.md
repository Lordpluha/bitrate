Every player page now has its own loading, error and not-found frames: pages that showed a blank panel while loading draw a skeleton shaped like the page, a failed page names what could not be loaded and offers a retry, a missing album or profile shows its not-found frame, and an unknown `/main/...` address shows "Page not found" inside the player instead of the full-screen 404.
