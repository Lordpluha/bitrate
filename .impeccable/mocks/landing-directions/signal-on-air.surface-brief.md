# Bitrate public landing

- Scope: public route `/`; visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience: listeners evaluating Bitrate before opening the browser player.
- Job: understand the listening experience at a glance and enter the player without marketing detours.
- Primary action: Open web player. Secondary action: Create account.
- Proof/content: an interactive, clearly labelled media-player demo; liked songs, recents, and continuity across return visits.
- Constraints: preserve `PRODUCT.md`, `brand.md`, Bitrate Purple, the standalone gradient mark, three-theme token model, WCAG 2.2 AA, 320px reflow, reduced motion, and honest feature claims. No pricing, testimonials, audience numbers, app-store claims, recommendations, lyrics, podcasts, or offline claims.
- Direction: Signal On Air — a calm modern broadcast-continuity surface with near-black fields, precise hairlines, tabular time data, restrained purple/blue active states, and original abstract album artwork.
- Memorable moment: the hero player owns the first viewport, and its uninterrupted playback line resolves into the persistent player at the final call to action.
- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/landing-directions/signal-on-air.png`.
- Unresolved: final brand wordmark and final type family remain open; security testing with Strix is explicitly deferred.
