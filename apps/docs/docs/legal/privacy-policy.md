This policy explains what personal data Bitrate collects when you use the listener app at bitrate.me and the artist portal at artists.bitrate.me, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have.

## 1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Vladyslav Tesliuk, Warsaw, Poland, a private individual ("Bitrate", "we"). Contact for privacy matters: tesluakevlad@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer because the GDPR does not require one for a service of this size and kind (Art. 37); please send every privacy question to the address above.

## 2. Who this applies to

Bitrate is for people aged **16 or over**. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; contact us if you believe a child has registered so we can delete the account.

Where we rely on legitimate interest you may object (section 9).

## 4. Data we receive from Google

If you choose **Sign in with Google**, Bitrate asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes **openid**, **email** and **profile**. From Google we receive your Google account id, your email address, whether Google has verified that address, and your name. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar, YouTube or any other Google data.

We use this data only to create your Bitrate account or find your existing one, to sign you in, and to contact you about your account. Your name is used to suggest your initial username. The Google token is used once, right after you sign in, to read the details above, and is then discarded: we do not store Google access or refresh tokens.

We do not sell data received from Google, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and do not let people read it except where you ask us to, for security purposes, or where the law requires it. We transfer it only to the providers in section 6 that we need to run the service, or where the law requires it.

Bitrate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy (https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke Bitrate's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have the data we received from Google deleted, delete your account or write to us (section 9).

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

Bitrate sets cookies that are **strictly necessary** to sign you in and keep your session secure: your session tokens, a short-lived token while you complete two-factor sign-in, and two short-lived cookies during social sign-in (one protects the sign-in from forgery, the other remembers that you accepted these documents while you are at the provider). They do not require consent. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

In the listener web app, our error-monitoring tool (Sentry) records error details and, for about one in ten sessions and for every session in which an error occurs, a session replay of how the interface was used. Replays mask all text and everything you type, and block images and media, so they show the layout of the page, clicks and navigation, not what you read or enter. We use replays only to find and fix bugs, on the basis of our legitimate interest in a working service. You can object at any time by writing to us (section 9).

## 7. Transfers outside the EEA

Our database and file storage stay in the EU. Some providers in section 6 are based in the USA. Where the provider is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework we rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision for it (GDPR Art. 45); otherwise we rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the Commission (Art. 46(2)(c)).

## 8. How long we keep data

- **Account data:** while your account exists, and deleted within 30 days after you delete it, except records we must keep for legal reasons.
- **Listening and search history:** while your account exists. You can clear your listening history at any time.
- **Audit logs, including IP addresses:** 12 months.
- **Moderation records and reports:** 3 years after the case is closed.
- **Records of the document versions you accepted:** while your account exists and for 3 years after, to prove acceptance.

## 9. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to: access your data (Art. 15), correct it (16), have it erased (17), restrict processing (18), receive it in a portable format (20), object to processing based on legitimate interest (21), and withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. Contact tesluakevlad@gmail.com. We aim to respond within one month.

You can change your profile and clear your listening history in the app, and artists can delete their account in the artist portal. Other requests, including a copy of your data or deletion of a listener account, are handled by email: write to us from the address on your account so we can confirm it is you.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (**UODO**), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, https://uodo.gov.pl, or with the authority in your own EU country.

## 10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures including hashed passwords, optional two-factor authentication, encrypted connections, access controls and account lockout after repeated failed sign-ins. No system is perfectly secure; we handle personal-data breaches as required by GDPR Art. 33–34.

## 11. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions that produce legal effects on you based solely on automated processing.

## 12. Changes

We will tell you about material changes before they take effect and record the version you accept. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
