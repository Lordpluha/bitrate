## 1. What Bitrate is

Bitrate is a service you use to host and share your music. Bitrate is not a record label, publisher or manager and
**does not acquire ownership of your recordings or compositions**.

## 2. Your content and warranties

2.1 "Your Content" means audio, artwork, titles and metadata you upload.

2.2 You warrant that, for everything you upload:

- you own it or hold all licences and permissions needed, including for any samples, featured performers and compositions;
- uploading it and granting the licence in section 3 does not infringe anyone's rights or any law;
- the information you provide about it (title, credits, explicit flag) is accurate.

2.3 You are responsible for rights in both the **recording (master)** and the **underlying composition**. If a collecting society
or another party controls part of those rights, you must have authority to upload.

## 3. Licence to Bitrate

You grant Bitrate a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, revocable licence to host, store, transcode, reproduce, display
and stream Your Content on Bitrate, and to create technical copies and previews needed to operate the service. Bitrate does not
sublicense Your Content to third parties except service providers acting on its behalf. The licence ends when you remove Your
Content or close your account, subject to reasonable backup periods and legal-retention obligations.

## 4. Removal and disputes

4.1 You may remove Your Content at any time.

4.2 Bitrate may remove or disable content after a valid notice under [Copyright and notice-and-action](./copyright-notice-and-action.md),
or where required by law or the [Community Guidelines](./community-guidelines.md). You can contest a decision using the counter-notice process.

4.3 Accounts that repeatedly upload infringing content may be terminated.
