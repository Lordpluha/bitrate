You can change your profile and clear your listening history yourself in the app.

For anything else, email **tesluakevlad@gmail.com** from the address on your account, with the subject **"Privacy request"** and the right you want to use. Under the GDPR you can ask to:

- get a copy of your data, or receive it in a portable format;
- correct or erase it;
- restrict processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interest.

We aim to answer within **one month**. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO, https://uodo.gov.pl) or to the authority in your own EU country.
