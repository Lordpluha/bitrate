To delete a listener account, email **tesluakevlad@gmail.com** from the address on your Bitrate account with the subject **"Delete my account"**. Writing from that address lets us confirm the request is yours.

We delete the account and the data linked to it, including a sign-in identity received from Google or Facebook, within **30 days**, and confirm by email. Records we must keep for legal reasons are kept only as long as the law requires.

If you signed in with a social account, you can also remove Bitrate from it:

- Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites → Bitrate → Remove.
- Google: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
