The support assistant answers from Bitrate's help articles and policies. If it cannot help, or you would rather talk to a person, say so in the chat, for example "I want to talk to a person". Your conversation goes to the Bitrate team with its full history, and the reply comes in the same chat.

For privacy requests, reports and complaints, you can always write to **tesluakevlad@gmail.com** instead.
