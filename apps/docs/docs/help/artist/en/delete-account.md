Artists delete their account themselves under **Settings** in the artist portal (artists.bitrate.me).

We delete the account and the data linked to it within **30 days**. Records we must keep for legal reasons, such as moderation records, are kept only as long as the law requires.

If you want a copy of your data first, ask before deleting: email **tesluakevlad@gmail.com** with the subject **"Privacy request"**.
